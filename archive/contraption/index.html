How to read the machine

  1. Top row: narrow pipes. Oil producers and the four sea chokepoints most trade squeezes through.
  2. Second row: gears, tolls and locked doors. Shipping, chips, food, tariffs, trade deals and sanctions that reroute flows.
  3. Middle row: the big wheels. US, China, EU, Japan, India and emerging markets.
  4. Lower rows: levers and gauges. Central banks react to the inflation gauge; rates, debt, jobs and spending feed back on each other.
Educational illustration only. Not financial advice and not a forecast.
